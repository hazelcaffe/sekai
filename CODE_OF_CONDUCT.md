# Code of conduct

Sekai is made by people who'd like working on it to be friendly. In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember there's a person on the other side.
- Welcome newcomers; questions are fine, and nobody needs to know everything.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. No insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect privacy: don't post other people's personal information, chats or logs without their consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who don't follow this. To report a problem, contact the
maintainers privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them confidentially).

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
